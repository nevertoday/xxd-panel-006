# XXD Panel 006 · 小主体纸感艺术书封

Turn each supplied photograph into a finished minimal paper-cover illustration. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes; every transformed frame must reduce the same subject to one small, concentrated, immediately recognisable vignette occupying about 10–20% of the design frame. Vast rough white or pale paper, sparse slightly unstable line, and a few complete acrylic flat shapes in no more than four source-derived colours create the quiet, playful confidence of an art-book cover rather than a generic children's cartoon.

Operational rules follow the shared XXD Panel workflow contract: four combinable modes; source-adaptive ordinary canvases; exact 50/50 paired geometry; linked or independent four-device wallpapers; copy and locale preflight; fresh generation jobs; privacy-preserving raster generation; and one fresh task directory per source and mode. Style-specific sections may refine aesthetics, copy hierarchy, and acceptance criteria but never override this contract.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes split exactly 50/50. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- Exact user pixels have highest priority, followed by an explicit ratio or destination. Otherwise ordinary modes adapt to the complete source: `top-bottom` = `W×2H`, `left-right` = `2W×H`, and `design-only` = `W×H`. Never impose the archived initial brief's 3:4 canvas as a silent default.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve identity, action, function, and emotion through at least three source-specific cues while keeping one compact subject vignette at roughly 10–20% of the frame. Sparse unstable line hints at structure; no more than four complete acrylic flat colours establish the subject; rough pale paper remains dominant. It is not tracing, pure line art, dense scenery, watercolour wash, pencil or crayon rendering, glossy vector art, photoreal painting, or 3D.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 006 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject or inseparable relation → preserve identity, action, function, emotion, and at least three defining cues → reduce it to one small concentrated vignette occupying about 10–20% of the design frame → let 80–90% remain rough white or pale paper → use sparse fine line with slight hand instability only to indicate structure → let a few complete acrylic flat shapes establish the subject → compress the source palette to no more than four principal colours → hint at setting with the minimum necessary line or colour plane → balance any concise copy through the surrounding whitespace like an art-book or independent-publishing cover**.

The childlike ease comes from radical editing, small scale, handmade uncertainty, and the source's recognisable identity—not from stock faces, mascots, suns, stars, or decorative doodles. Art-book covers, independent publishing, paper illustration, and picture-book restraint are construction influences rather than templates.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the tiny vignette, defining cues, colour blocks, whitespace relationship, or copy. Also reject a subject larger than the intended 10–20% without explicit reason, busy backgrounds, evenly scattered doodles, pure line art, translucent wash, pencil or crayon texture, smooth vector polish, commercial-cartoon styling, photoreal shading, 3D volume, and decorative symbols not earned by the source.

## 006 visual system

### Small concentrated subject, high recognition

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across contour, pose/orientation, function, proportion, material, colour, opening, connection, overlap, negative shape, or relationship distance.
- Reduce the principal subject or inseparable relation to one compact vignette occupying roughly 10–20% of the design frame. Simplification removes complexity but preserves identity, action, function, and emotion at first glance.
- People keep pose, direction, clothing mass, and relational distance; animals keep body rhythm and head direction; plants keep growth gesture; architecture keeps skyline and defining openings; objects and vehicles keep functional silhouette and negative shape; landscapes keep one source-specific terrain or spatial relation.
- Keep one principal subject or inseparable relationship. Any secondary mark must help identify it or minimally imply setting; it may not become a second focus.

### Vast rough-paper whitespace

- Keep roughly 80–90% of the frame as rough white, warm white, or a source-compatible pale paper field. Whitespace is the main spatial material, not an empty area waiting for decoration.
- Place the vignette with quiet asymmetry and deliberate counterweight. It may sit low, high, off-centre, or near an edge when that placement follows source gesture and copy, but it must remain concentrated.
- Use at most a few short lines or one restrained colour plane to imply ground, horizon, enclosure, direction, or atmosphere. Do not reconstruct the whole scene.
- Preserve calm, lightness, play, and poetic tension. Reject borders, panels, background patterns, scattered icons, or repeated motifs that consume the paper.

### Fine unstable line, complete acrylic shapes

- Use thin, slightly unstable hand line only to indicate essential structure, boundary, connection, texture, or recognition. It should feel human without becoming scratchy, hesitant noise.
- Let complete opaque acrylic flat shapes establish the subject's visual mass. Colour blocks are clear and integral, with hand-painted deposits and gently irregular edges.
- Line and colour have separate jobs: line suggests structure; colour constructs the subject. Neither may dominate into pure line art or a painted full-field illustration.
- Preserve paper grain and small manual irregularities. Reject coloured pencil, crayon, wax, pastel, watercolour bloom, ink wash, thick impasto, realistic modelling, smooth digital illustration, and 3D.

### No more than four source-derived colours

- Extract and compress the source palette to no more than four principal colours, excluding the rough paper field. Each colour must have a distinct structural or semantic job.
- Keep blocks vivid, complete, and restrained. A colour may identify clothing, material, opening, face, sign, terrain, or functional point, but never become generic decoration.
- Preserve source atmosphere without reproducing every hue. Prefer fewer colours when recognition remains strong.
- Avoid rainbow palettes, transparent gradients, muddy mixing, neon glow, corporate brand systems, and uniform beige filtering.

## Copy and typography belong to 006

006 text behaves like sparse book-cover typography balanced against the tiny vignette and large paper field—not a headline wall, UI label, data display, or detached advertising overlay.

- Automatic copy derives one concise title, theme word, object name, place, or short phrase from identity, visible action, function, relation, or known context without inventing a story.
- Prefer one restrained title plus zero to two very small supporting elements. A number, place, or year appears only when supplied or reliably established.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: generic lifestyle words that survive on another source must be rewritten.

Typography has its own 006 construction:

- **Role:** a sparse cover line that counterbalances the small illustration and gives the paper field editorial rhythm.
- **Voice:** quiet, light, childlike without childishness, poetic, relaxed, and refined.
- **Construction:** one concise title in a simple handmade or understated book typeface, with at most two smaller supporting lines. Do not force one proportion across scripts.
- **Placement:** use the large whitespace as a compositional counterweight—above, below, or beside the vignette—without crowding it or covering key cues.
- **Material:** lightly imperfect dry ink, opaque print, or subtle letterpress consistent with paper and acrylic; never crayon, glossy digital effects, or oversized commercial display type.
- **Script:** use a native hand-made editorial equivalent for the resolved locale. Preserve natural Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Latin construction rather than imitating Latin handwriting across every script.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.

Do not ask for a second confirmation solely because the route changes. This does not authorise changing credentials, providers, billing, accounts, or global settings.

## Fresh-task and source boundary

## Composition commands

```bash
# Source-adaptive top-bottom
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --plan --layout top-bottom --source photo.png
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout top-bottom

# Source-adaptive left-right
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --plan --layout left-right --source photo.png
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout left-right

# Design-only
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout design-only

# One wallpaper, repeated separately for all four devices
python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --design phone.png --out wallpaper-phone.png \
  --layout design-only --size 1440x3200
```

`--size WIDTHxHEIGHT` overrides `--canvas` and source adaptation. Generate the design at the exact planned aspect; if exact pixels are unsupported, use a closest supported same-aspect bitmap and resample proportionally without cropping. Audit with the same layout used to build the result.

## Acceptance gate

Before accepting each result verify:

- Mode, exact pixels, source-adaptive formula, split axis, seam, and output count are correct.
- Visible photography is faithful and type-free; source-hidden outputs contain no source photograph or seam.
- The simplified subject remains recognisable through at least three source-specific cues and preserves the principal action or inseparable relationship.
- The subject remains immediately recognisable through at least three source-specific cues while the complete vignette occupies roughly 10–20% of the design frame unless the user explicitly directs otherwise.
- Rough white or pale paper clearly dominates roughly 80–90% of the frame. The whitespace feels intentionally balanced, not empty, cropped by accident, or filled with decoration.
- Sparse fine line indicates only essential structure, boundary, connection, texture, or identity; it does not become pure line art or nervous scratchwork.
- A few complete opaque acrylic flat shapes establish the subject, with paper grain, hand-painted deposits, and gently irregular edges. No watercolour bloom, coloured-pencil, crayon, pastel, wash, impasto, glossy vector, or 3D treatment appears.
- The source-derived palette contains no more than four principal colours excluding paper, and every colour has a clear recognition or structural role.
- Environmental hints remain minimal, and the overall result is quiet, playful, light, poetic, and editorial rather than commercially cute, e-commerce styled, or template-like.
- Automatic copy is concise, grounded, and 006-specific; exact user wording remains verbatim. All rendered text is accurate and native to the resolved locale; text-free output contains no text or pseudo-text.
- Typography reads as sparse art-book-cover composition balanced through the whitespace. It never looks like a generic bold poster title, UI label, oversized advertising copy, or detachable digital overlay.
- Every wallpaper is separately recomposed, respects safe regions, contains no system UI, and is not a crop of another device result. A linked pack shares one family without drifting from the source.
- Every delivered PNG was newly generated for this invocation and lives in its fresh task directory.
